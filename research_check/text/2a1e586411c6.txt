 The Bank of Cyprus Nicosia Marathon is the official marathon of Cyprus’ capital city and one of the island’s leading sporting events. Held annually in Nicosia, the event attracts thousands of runners and visitors from Cyprus and abroad, offering a unique experience that combines sport, culture, history, and hospitality in the heart of the capital. 
 The event features six race categories suitable for all ages and fitness levels. Participants have the opportunity to run through the streets of Nicosia, passing by historic landmarks, cultural attractions, and modern districts of the capital. 
 Starting and finishing at the iconic Eleftheria Square, the race route offers a memorable running experience that blends contemporary urban life with the rich history and cultural heritage of Nicosia. The event also places strong emphasis on social contribution, inclusivity, and sustainability, while supporting charitable organisations and community initiatives. 
 The Bank of Cyprus Nicosia Marathon has evolved into an internationally recognised sports tourism event that promotes Nicosia as a modern destination for athletics, culture, and hospitality, strengthening the international profile of Cyprus’ capital city.
