HOW TO Buy your tickets Select a date Select a date which you find suitable for your tour and check if it's available for you preferred time through our booking calendar. Book it ‍ Add your name, email, telephone number and location of your stay (if you're using our zoo shuttle) and secure your ticket with SSL encrypted payment gateways. Your exploration begins! After you complete your booking, you'll receive an email with exact details on pickup location and unique ticket which you can scan at the zoo entrance and start your journey. Transfer Bus Our direct transfer bus runs from Paphos everyday during the summer months (April – October) and 3 times per week during the winter months (November – March). This service has pick ups from the majority of the Hotels in the Paphos region and requires a reservation that can be done by selecting the direct transfer ticket above.
