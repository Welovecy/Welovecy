 The place Val's Place, Pano Gialia 
 A family-run festival and retreat venue set between forested hills and the sea, near Polis Chrysochous in the Paphos district. 
 The land has been tended by the same family since the late 1980s — first as a village café, and since 2015 as Val's Place: a home for music, gatherings and retreats, with an organic garden, open-air stages and camping grounds under the stars. AWYNO 2027 is one of the gatherings the venue is hosting in summer 2027.

 Getting there Finding your way 
 The location Val's Place Pano Gialia, 8875 Paphos, Cyprus A rural setting in the Polis Chrysochous area of the Paphos district — close to the forest and the sea. 
 By air Paphos International Airport is the nearest airport. From there, Pano Gialia is reached by road towards the Polis area. Transfer and shuttle details soon. 
 By road Pano Gialia lies in the Polis area of the Paphos district. Parking on site is free; directions and car-sharing information will be shared closer to the festival.

 Camping & stay Rest under the Cypriot sky Future 7-day, 2-day, 3-day and 4-day tickets include camping — please bring your own tent . Val's Place has its own camping grounds, and during the festival they are reserved for AWYNO guests. Only 400 camping slots are available across all ticket types. Please bring your own tent. Camper vans are not allowed. Wherever you rest, we ask everyone to treat the land with care and leave it as beautiful as we found it. 
 Camping Included with every full pass — please bring your own tent. No camper vans.

 Families welcome 
 Children at AWYNO Festival ❤️ 
 Families are very welcome. Kids aged 0–11 go free with a free child ticket, teens 12–17 need a ticket, and all under-18s must be with a parent or guardian at all times. 
 ID or age may be checked at the gate. 
 Every child gets a wristband. Please write a parent's phone number on it. 
 If a child gets lost, the meeting point is the Info Point. 
 Please note: the music plays around the clock, day and night, and the camping areas are close to the stages. Please bring ear protection for your little ones. 
 First aid is on site. 
 Pets 
 Pets and dogs 🐾 
 Pets are allowed, but we kindly ask you to leave them at home if you can. June is very hot in Cyprus, and with big crowds, loud music and other animals around, we want to avoid any stress, possible fights or uncomfortable situations. Our wish is for everyone, animals included, to be safe and comfortable, so you can fully relax and enjoy the festival experience. 
 For AWYNO 2028 we're planning a dedicated pet area with activities. 🐾 
 If you do bring a dog 
 Dogs must be on a lead at all times. 
 Never leave a dog alone in a tent or a car. 
 Please clean up after your dog. You are responsible for any damage. 
 Please bring up-to-date vaccination records. 
 No dogs in front of the stages. 
 Staff may ask for an aggressive or distressed dog to be removed from the site. 
 Thank you for understanding ❤️ 
 FAQ Questions & answers More answers will be added as details are confirmed. 
 When and where is AWYNO 2027? 5–11 June 2027 at Val's Place, Pano Gialia, 8875 Paphos, Cyprus. See the venue map and location . How long is the festival? Seven days in total (5–11 June 2027), with seven days of artist performances across four stages — Cosmos Stage, Solar Stage, Oasis Stage and Ember Stage — and daily morning activities from 6–10 June at the Oasis Stage and Workshop Venue. When do tickets go on sale? Tickets are on sale now. Get your tickets on the Tickets page. Is there camping or accommodation? Yes — camping for the full week is included with every full pass (Full Pass + Camping). Day tickets don't include camping. Only 400 camping slots are available across all ticket types. Please bring your own tent. Camper vans are not allowed. Please bring your own tent. Are there day tickets? There are 7-day, 4-day, 3-day and 2-day tickets with camping. See all ticket types and buy on the Tickets page. Is the festival cashless? Yes, AWYNO is planned as a cashless festival. Wallet top-up details are coming soon. What is the age policy? Families are welcome. Children aged 0–11 enter free with a free child ticket, teens 12–17 need a ticket, and all under-18s must be accompanied by a parent or guardian at all times. Valid ID may be checked at the entrance. See the children note for on-site rules. Can I bring a pet? Pets are allowed, but we kindly ask you to leave them at home if you can, because of the June heat and crowds. A pet area is planned for 2028. Read the pets note . What should I bring? A full packing list will be shared closer to the festival. How does my ticket give back? AWYNO EVENTS will give 50% of ticket revenue to charity. Read our mission . I have another question.
