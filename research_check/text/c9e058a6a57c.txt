 Welcome to the website of the UNIQUE EDUTAINMENT CENTRE of the future in Cyprus COSMOPORT ! 
A perfect place for education, entertainment and leisure for people of all ages in Limassol, located opposite the Molos promenade. Here you can find many exciting Interactive zones: VR attractions, interactive installations, kinetic and interactive sandbox, various workshops & masterclasses, drawing, VR Piloting and much more . Everything is presented in an ultra-modern format. Engaging mechanics of multimedia games develop logic, thinking, imagination, dexterity, coordination and other skills of the child. A fun and rewarding experience for the whole family awaits!
 We do not only celebrate parties. You can come alone or in company and just play!
