 2026 HIGHLIGHTS ABOUT THE CYPRUS DIASPORA FORUM® The Cyprus Diaspora Forum® is a leading global business platform connecting Cyprus with its worldwide diaspora, international investors, business leaders, innovators, policymakers, academics, entrepreneurs, cultural ambassadors, and emerging talent. More than an annual conference, it is a catalyst for collaboration, investment, innovation, talent repatriation, and long-term strategic partnerships that contribute to Cyprus' economic, social, and cultural development. 
 ​ 
 Recognised as the most comprehensive event of its kind, the Forum offers a unique cross-sector outlook on the major industry and economic pillars of Cyprus. Bringing together leaders from finance, fintech, technology, artificial intelligence, research and innovation, healthcare, education, real estate, tourism, legal services, wealth management, family offices, entrepreneurship, startups, culture, sustainability, and public policy, it provides an unparalleled opportunity to understand the full breadth of Cyprus' economic landscape under one roof. 
 ​ 
 One of the Forum's defining missions is to strengthen the relationship between Cyprus and its global diaspora while creating meaningful opportunities for talented Cypriots abroad to reconnect with the island. By facilitating conversations around careers, entrepreneurship, research, investment, and innovation, the Forum actively supports talent repatriation, encouraging experienced professionals, academics, entrepreneurs, and innovators to return, invest, collaborate, or build their next chapter in Cyprus. 
 ​ 
 The Forum also serves as a powerful commercial platform for Cyprus. It provides local businesses, startups, SMEs, and established organisations with the opportunity to showcase their products, services, technologies, and innovations to an influential audience of international investors, corporate leaders, decision-makers, family offices, government representatives, and potential clients. By creating direct access to global networks, the Forum helps businesses generate new partnerships, attract investment, expand internationally, and unlock new commercial opportunities. 
 ​ 
 Equally important is the Forum's commitment to inspiring the next generation. Senior school students and university students are given a rare opportunity to engage directly with internationally recognised business leaders, entrepreneurs, academics, innovators, and policymakers. Through keynote presentations, panel discussions, networking sessions, and informal conversations, young people gain invaluable access to mentors whose guidance can shape future careers, broaden aspirations, and foster the next generation of Cypriot leadership. 
 ​ 
 Since its inception, the Forum has evolved into one of the most significant international gatherings dedicated to Cyprus, bringing together the island's global network of talent and influence while welcoming international partners who share a vision for the country's future. It is a place where ideas become action, introductions become partnerships, and conversations lead to measurable impact. 
 ​ 
 Supported by leading national institutions and strategic partners, the Cyprus Diaspora Forum® has established itself as a trusted platform where government, academia, industry, investors, and the global diaspora work together to advance Cyprus' international competitiveness, innovation ecosystem, investment landscape, and economic prosperity. 
 ​ 
 The Forum also celebrates Cypriot excellence through the prestigious CYDIA Awards®, recognising outstanding individuals whose achievements have elevated Cyprus on the world stage. Alongside its focus on business and investment, the Forum supports initiatives that promote Cyprus' culture, heritage, creativity, and identity, recognising that economic success and cultural pride go hand in hand. JOHN KIRIAKOU CIA Torture Whistleblower USA John Kiriakou is a journalist and former CIA counterterrorism officer, as well as a former senior investigator for the US Senate Foreign Relations Committee and a former counterterrorism consultant for ABC News. In 2002, he was involved in the capture in Pakistan of Abu Zubaydah, who was at the time believed to be the third-ranking official in Al-Qaeda. In 2007, Kiriakou became a whistleblower by publicly exposing the CIA’s torture programme, stating in an interview with ABC News that the torture of detainees was official US government policy and that it had been authorised by then President George W. Bush. He subsequently became the sixth whistleblower prosecuted under the Espionage Act during the Obama administration — legislation originally intended to punish espionage. As a result, he served 23 months in prison. Kiriakou is the author of eight books, including The Reluctant Spy: My Secret Life in the CIA’s War on Terror; Doing Time Like a Spy: How the CIA Taught Me to Survive and Thrive in Prison; The Convenient Terrorist: Abu Zubaydah and the Weird Wonderland of America’s Secret Wars; and The CIA Insider’s Guide to the Iran Crisis.
