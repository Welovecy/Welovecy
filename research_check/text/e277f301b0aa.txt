 Admission Tickets 
 TUESDAY - THURSDAY 
 €7.00 
 WEEKENDS & PUBLIC HOLIDAYS 
 €9.00 
 Adults do not pay entrance fee. 
 Admission tickets can by purchased from the reception of each level/section of the Park. The tickets are valid only for one level/section of the Park.  
 There is an additional charge of 3 euro to play in all sections of the park.  
 There are age restrictions on certain games. 
 School & Kindergarten visits 
 For schools & kindergartens arrangements can be made for visits in other times than the normal opening hours. Please call for pricing and other info.
