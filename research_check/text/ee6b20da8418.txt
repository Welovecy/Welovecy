 APHRODITE HALF MARATHON, 10KM & 5KM – Sunday 18 th April 2027 
 The Aphrodite Half Marathon, a premier running event in Cyprus, is an experience designed to captivate both local and international runners. Organized by Running in Cyprus, the team behind 
 the renowned TUI Cyprus Marathon and the community-focused Lakatameia Energy Run , this event benefits from more than 35 years of expertise in the running industry. 
 First held in 1994, the race moves to Geroskipou in 2027 under the auspices of the Municipality of Ierokipia. It stands out for its fast, coastal route by the sea, starting from Geroskipou Stadium in 
 Ierokipia, Pafos on the beautiful west coast of Cyprus. 
 The event is designed to make every runner feel special, with beautiful custom medals for each race, trophies to the top three men and women in various age groups, including the 60+ category 
 (in Half Marathon & 10km Race). The event attracts a diverse audience, including avid runners, gym-goers, and corporate walkers. 
 With solid expertise and a bold vision, the Aphrodite Half Marathon aims to encourage mass participation, promote a healthy and active lifestyle, and provide a truly memorable experience 
 for all who take part. 
 Start time/End time: Sunday 18 th April – 8:00 a.m. till 11:00 a.m. (Half Marathon, 10km race and 5km)
