 Activities for families 
 Whether a grown-up kid or just a kid Cyprus has all those ‘ingredients’ that comprise a beautiful and exciting fairytale or an adventurous and fascinating time-capsule ride through 11,000 years of history and civilisation!  Step into a medieval castle and learn about the royal wedding that took place there hundreds of years ago! Walk into the world of a Neolithic settlement and learn how people lived during the Stone Age! Hike in the woods and find the hidden waterfalls! Feeling brave? Feeling Adventurous? Want to try something new? Try the steep hair-raising water slides in one of the water parks! Or the speed zipe-line in the forest adventure park! Or fast karts drive! Want to take an extraordinary ride? Try a donkey or a camel ride! Love animals? Visit a zoo! Feeling sporty? Try golfing or mini-golfing or bowling! Just a little toddler? Have fun with mum and dad in a playground!  Whichever the age come and join in the fun in Cyprus!
