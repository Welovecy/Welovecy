 FAQ 
 How to get to the park? 
 Getting here is easy! We’re located on Nissi Avenue, right in the heart of Ayia Napa, making us easily accessible from the town centre. If you’re staying a little further away and don’t have a car, no worries — you can reach us by bus. You’ll nd the daily OSEA bus timetables below to help you plan your journey. Coming by car? We’ve got you covered with free parking for up to 300 cars  
 How can I pay during my visit? 
 We accept cash and credit card payments, and please note that we only accept Euros (€). Kindly keep in mind that tokens are non-refundable. 
 Will you be open all year? 
 We usually operate until the end of October. During the winter months, November to January, we move to Nicosia for the festive season as Christmas Land. Planning to visit? Come see us there! Keep an eye on our social media for the exact dates and latest updates.
