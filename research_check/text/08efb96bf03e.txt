 The Museum was established by Andreas Nicolaou and is a personal endeavor which started in 1980. 
 Today, this collection comprises of more than 450 motorcycles. At the Museum you can see motorcycles dated between 1914 and 1983, such as (AJS, MV AQUSTA, MATCHLESS, NORTON, BSA, TRIUMPH, ARIEL, BMW, JAMES, MOTO GUZZI , ROYAL ENFIELD and not only ! All of the motorcycles are lovingly cared by Andreas who entertains visitors with the potted history of each bike. BOOK YOUR VISIT Motorcycle Brands That You Will Expect To See:
