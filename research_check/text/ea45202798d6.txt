 Event Details 
 Event Details 
 Experience the excitement of Cyprus’ largest and most celebrated carnival! Limassol Carnival 2027 welcomes visitors from around the world to discover the vibrant traditions, creativity, and warm hospitality of Limassol. 
 For eleven days, the city comes alive with colorful festivities, music, dance, spectacular parades, and entertainment for all ages. The celebrations reach their grand finale on Sunday, 14 March 2027, when the famous Grand Carnival Parade fills the streets with impressive floats, imaginative costumes, and an unforgettable festive atmosphere. 
 Whether you are visiting Limassol for the first time or returning to relive the experience, the carnival offers a unique opportunity to immerse yourself in Cypriot culture and enjoy one of the Mediterranean’s most iconic annual events. 
 Dates 
 4 – 14 March 2027 
 Location 
 Limassol, Cyprus 
 Information 
 For updates and visitor information, please visit the official website of the Municipality of Limassol. 
 Join thousands of visitors and locals in celebrating a tradition that brings together culture, entertainment, and community spirit in the heart of Limassol. 🎭✨
