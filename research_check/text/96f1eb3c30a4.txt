 Accessible Services 
 The A. G. Leventis Gallery is committed to providing an inclusive and accessible experience for all visitors. 
 Building accessibility 
 Step-free entrance suitable for wheelchairs and strollers Elevators provide access to all floors Wheelchair-accessible restrooms Seating is available in galleries and public areas Basement car park available Wheelchair is available to borrow during your visit Service animals are welcome throughout the building 
 Guided Tours & Interpretations

 Audio & Visual Tours 
 Audio Tours 
 Download ‘Smartify’ app from the App Store or Google Play to enjoy a self-guided tour and uncover the richness of European art at your own pace. Discover the A. G. Leventis Gallery and explore the stories behind the Cyprus, Greek, Paris and Gold Collections. 
 Visual tours in Cypriot Sign Language 
 The A. G. Leventis Gallery is committed to making art accessible to all. In collaboration with the School for the Deaf, the Gallery offers short  guided tours in Cypriot Sign Language. By scanning the QR code on selected artwork labels, visitors can access these videos and explore the Gallery’s collections while discovering the richness of art history. 
 Groups & Guided Tours 
 Free Public Tours 
 The Gallery regularly offers free guided tours of its permanent collections and temporary exhibitions. Dates and times are announced on the website and through the media. 
 Self-Guided Group Visits 
 Groups may explore the Gallery independently using audio guides, available from the Audio Guide Desk in the lobby. 
 Private Tours & Lectures 
 Private tours and lectures can be arranged for groups or individuals wishing to explore specific themes or exhibitions. 
 Available tour themes 
 Highlights of the A. G. Leventis Gallery Thematic guided tours upon request Temporary exhibitions

 Visitor Guidelines 
 Do’s 
 Take photographs without flash Sketch using a pencil Share your photos on social media 
 Don’ts 
 Use flash or tripods for photography Touch artworks or exhibits Bring food or drinks into the Gallery Smoke inside the premises Wear hats or sunglasses inside exhibition spaces Bring animals onto the premises (only if it’s a service dog it’s allowed) Please keep mobile phones on silent and refrain from making or receiving calls during your visit 
 Shop 
 Discover The AGLG Shop, the Gallery’s store, where art continues its journey beyond the exhibition spaces. Browse a carefully curated selection of publications, art-inspired gifts, unique souvenirs, and beautifully designed objects that celebrate creativity and culture. Every item has been thoughtfully chosen to extend your gallery experience and offer a meaningful keepsake or the perfect gift. Visit the AGLG E-Shop and explore our collection before your visit—or find the perfect piece to bring the A. G. Leventis Gallery experience home, wherever you are.
