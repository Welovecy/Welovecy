SUPPORT The Cyprus Association of Sports Medicine will offer full medical support throughout the event. Post-race physiotherapy care/advise will also be offered for those athletes needing such treatment. The course will be covered by Ambulances & Doctors. All participants are strongly encouraged to take out suitable personal cover for injury, damage or loss of equipment. The organisers will not be liable for such incidents as a result of either participation in, or attendance at the event. Weather The average temperature in late February/early March in Paphos is 16°C with lowest temperature being 9°C and highest 22°C. Humidity is low. Time Limits & Rules A 6 hour time limit will be imposed on the TUI Cyprus Marathon event and a 3 hour limit for the TUI Cyprus Half-Marathon. The event will be conducted in accordance with the Cyprus Amateur Athletics Federation (KOEAS), and the International Amateur Athletic Federation (IAAF) rules. Finish Line Services After the finisher’s medal, ample quantities of beer, bananas, electrolyte and mineral water will be offered free of charge at the finish line.
