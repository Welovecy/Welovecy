The gallery's permanent collection of paintings consists of pieces by eminent Cypriot and other artists, representative of contemporary artistic talent on the island. PLEASE NOTE THAT PRIOR TO NEW EXHIBITIONS OPENING, THE GALLERY MAY BE CLOSED FOR SHORT PERIODS FOR THE SET-UP.  Europe Square
