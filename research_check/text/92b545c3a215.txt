 Tourist Information Offices 
 The Deputy Ministry of Tourism has local Information Offices in all main tourist areas. Our experienced colleagues will be happy to assist you and provide you with information and suggestions to make your stay a pleasurable and rewarding experience. For personal and telephone inquiries please visit or contact our Offices: Opening Hours:  08.30-16.00 Monday-Friday Lefkosia (Nicosia) Aristokyprou 11, Laiki Geitonia (east of Eleftheria’s Square) 1011 Lefkosia Tel: + 357 22674264 Lemesos (Limassol) Plateia Michalis Kakogiannis, Old port, Building THK1 3047 Lemesos Tel: + 357 25362756 Platres  Olympou 4C 4820 Pano Platres Tel : + 357 25421316 Larnaka (From 15/4/2026 the Larnaca Information Office will remain closed for renovation until until the  mid – December).
