The Troodos mountains are a different Cyprus: pine forest, cool air and villages of stone houses and wooden balconies. You'll need a car or a tour, and a full day.

## Choose your villages

### Omodos
A wine village with a **big village square**, lined with cafés, and its own **village museums**. A good first stop coming up from Limassol or Paphos. [Omodos](/destinations/troodos)

### Platres
The classic **mountain base**, among the pines, with places to eat and stay. A natural lunch stop. [Platres](/destinations/troodos)

### Kakopetria
On the north side of the mountains, the **mountain resort** of **Kakopetria** has an old quarter beside the river, and **village museums** too. [Kakopetria](/destinations/troodos)

Our tip: give each village a purpose. A square and lunch in one, a church in another, a walk in the third.

## Add a walk

- **Artemis trail:** a **7 km circular** walk around the top of the mountains, about **2.5 to 3 hours**. Follow the **signs for the Artemis Trail off the Troodos to Prodromos road**.
- **Towards Kakopetria:** a signed trail from Kannoures finishes at **Agios Nikolaos tis Stegis**, one of the painted churches, or carries on into **Kakopetria** village.

## Add a painted church

Ten churches in the Troodos are a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Agios Nikolaos tis Stegis is just outside Kakopetria. See the [Troodos painted churches route](/guides/troodos-painted-churches-route).

## Winter

In winter, **temperatures below freezing are not uncommon** in the mountains, and there can be snow. Check the roads and the trail status before you go, and pack layers. See [skiing on Mount Olympus](/guides/skiing-on-mount-olympus).

## Good to know

- Church, museum and winery opening times aren't always published. Check each one before you set off.
- Mountain roads are slower than they look on the map. See [How far is it?](/distances) for real drive times.
