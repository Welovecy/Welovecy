Easter is the biggest celebration of the year in Cyprus, bigger than Christmas. If you're here for it, you'll see the island at its most itself.

## The 2027 dates

| Day | Date |
|---|---|
| Orthodox Good Friday | Friday 30 April |
| Orthodox Easter Sunday | Sunday 2 May |
| Orthodox Easter Monday | Monday 3 May |
| Easter Tuesday (banks closed) | Tuesday 4 May |

**Western Easter** falls earlier, on **Sunday 28 March 2027**.

Shops, banks and bus timetables can all change around Easter, so check each one separately. See [Cyprus public holidays 2027](/guides/cyprus-public-holidays-2027).

## The traditions

### Good Friday: the Epitaphios
On Good Friday, the **Epitaphios**, the symbolic tomb of Christ, is **decorated with flowers** in every church and carried in procession in the evening.

### Red eggs
**Colouring eggs** is part of Easter. They're cracked against each other, and the last unbroken egg wins.

### Flaounes
Look out for **flaounes** in bakeries in the run-up to Easter. These traditional pastries are filled with **cheese and eggs**, often with **raisins and mint**, and they're a staple of the Orthodox Easter period. See [traditional Cypriot food](/guides/traditional-cypriot-food-and-meze).

## Visiting a service

Visitors are welcome at many services. Dress modestly, follow local instructions around the processions, and ask locally which church to go to.

## Good to know

Exact 2027 service times and town events are announced locally nearer the time.
