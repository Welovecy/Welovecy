Polis and Latchi are the gateway to the Akamas, and slower and greener than the rest of the coast. Here's how to spend a day.

## Latchi harbour

**Latchi** is a small harbour **just west of Polis**. It's where the **boats to the Blue Lagoon** leave from, and a **row of fish tavernas** lines the waterfront. Walk the harbour first, then decide where to swim and where to eat. [Latchi](/destinations/akamas)

## The beaches

- **Latchi beach** runs **either side of the harbour**. It's a **mix of sand and pebbles**, with a **taverna, toilets and parking** listed.
- **Yialos** is a walk further **west** from the harbour.

Choose by who needs shade, toilets or lunch close by. Check the flags and lifeguard cover on the day.

## Out on the water

The Blue Lagoon boat trip is the highlight for most visitors. See [Blue Lagoon boat trips from Latchi](/guides/blue-lagoon-boat-trips-from-latchi).

## Getting there without a car

- **Route 645:** **Paphos (Karavella)** to **Polis**.
- **Route 622:** **Polis**, through **Latchi**, to the **Baths of Aphrodite**.

Check both with Pafos Buses for your date, and especially the last bus back to Paphos.

## A day plan

1. **Morning:** the bus or drive up from Paphos, then a coffee in Polis.
2. **Late morning:** a boat trip from Latchi, or a swim.
3. **Lunch:** fish by the harbour.
4. **Afternoon:** the beach, then the Baths of Aphrodite on the way west, if you have the energy.
