You don't need a car for a great week in Cyprus. You just need the right base. Cyprus has no trains or metro, but every major town has buses, both within town and between towns, and urban taxis run on meters. Some summer resort services run later into the evening, too.

The trick is choosing a hotel where the things you need every day are a short walk away.

## The best bases without a car

### Kato Paphos
The easiest place on the island to go car-free. Airport bus **612** runs straight from Paphos Airport to Tombs of the Kings station, and from there bus **615** runs along the coast to Coral Bay. The harbour, the mosaics and the seafront are all walkable. See [Paphos Airport to Paphos and Coral Bay](/guides/paphos-airport-to-paphos-and-coral-bay).

### Central Larnaca
Small, flat and right by the airport, just **6 km** away. Route **425** links the airport with the hotels, Mackenzie and Finikoudes. The seafront, the Church of Saint Lazarus and the market are all close together, and Intercity buses leave Finikoudes for Ayia Napa, Limassol and Nicosia.

### Ayia Napa
Compact, with a busy centre, and the beaches run in a line along the coast. Stay near the centre or Nissi Avenue and you can walk to most of what you'll want. Local OSEA buses link the resort with Protaras and Paralimni.

### Central Protaras
A seafront promenade of **more than 4 km** links the Agios Nikolaos fishing shelter with Fig Tree Bay, and much of resort life happens along it. Stay close to that stretch and you can walk to the beach, restaurants and shops. Kapparis and the far end of Pernera are quieter, but further from everything.

## Where a car really helps

- **Troodos villages and the painted churches.** Buses are limited and the roads are mountain roads.
- **Akamas, Lara and the remote beaches.**
- **Villas up in the hills** above any resort.

Staying in one of these? Plan a day or two of car hire, or book a tour, rather than a whole week.

## Before you book: the 3-minute check

Open the hotel's page on We Love Cyprus and look at:

1. **The nearest beach,** with the walking time.
2. **The nearest supermarket and pharmacy.**
3. **The nearest bus stop, and where its buses go.** Check the return, not just the outward trip, and when the last bus leaves.

If the walk to the bus stop or the supermarket is more than 15 minutes, budget for taxis.
