Protaras isn't one beach, it's a coastline of small ones. The best approach: pick your part of town, then choose between two or three beaches within a short walk.

## Fig Tree Bay: the main event

Central Protaras's best-known beach, named after a fig tree said to have grown here for centuries. The official listing includes **toilets, changing rooms, parking, lifeguards from April to October**, and a **special beach wheelchair** at the lifeguard post. The seafront promenade runs here from the Agios Nikolaos fishing shelter. [Fig Tree Bay](/places/fig-tree-bay)

## Konnos Bay: the cove everyone photographs

On the road between Cape Greco and Protaras, Konnos is a **200-metre** cove of pale sand below pine-covered slopes. Lovely for snorkelling. Arrive early in summer. [Konnos Bay](/places/konnos)

## Pernera and Louma

North of the centre, Pernera has its own small beaches, including **Pernera** and **Louma**, both on the municipality's official beach list. It's a quieter base with an easy walk into Protaras along the coast. [Pernera neighbourhood](/destinations/protaras/pernera)

## Kapparis and Agia Triada

Further north again, **Kapparis** has its own sandy bay, and **Agia Triada** sits beside the small church of the same name. Both are municipal beaches, and quieter than anything in central Protaras. [Kapparis neighbourhood](/destinations/protaras/kapparis)

## Which one for you?

- **Everything on hand, families, first visit:** Fig Tree Bay.
- **The photo, the snorkel:** Konnos.
- **A quiet base with walkable beaches:** Pernera.
- **Away from it all:** Kapparis.

## Good to know

- Lifeguard months are published per beach, but check the flag and the tower when you arrive.
- Need step-free access? Fig Tree Bay's beach wheelchair is kept at the lifeguard post. Ask the lifeguard.
- Going further? Try [best beaches in Ayia Napa](/guides/best-beaches-in-ayia-napa), 15 minutes down the coast.
