Nissi is the beach on the postcards, and it lives up to them.

## The essentials

- **Where:** **3.3 km west** of Ayia Napa's centre.
- **Size:** about **600 metres** long.
- **The sand:** **fine and golden**, in a **natural bay**.
- **The water:** **shallow**, and very clear.
- **The name:** "nissi" means island. The beach takes its name from the **small islet** just offshore.

## The islet and the sand lane

**Nissi Bay**, listed separately by the tourist board, has the islet and the **sand lane** that leads out towards it. On calm days people wade across. Check the flags first, and how the sand lane looks that day, as conditions change.

## Facilities

The official listing includes **toilets, changing rooms** and **disabled access**. Lifeguards are usually on duty **from April to October**, though that's the season, not a promise for every day. Check the flag and the tower when you arrive.

## Getting there

The listing describes getting there **on foot, by bike and by car**. It's a long-ish walk from the centre, but easy from the hotels along Nissi Avenue. Open [Nissi Beach](/places/nissi) to see the nearest bus stops.

## Our tip

Nissi is lively, with music and events in summer. If you're in a group, agree a meeting point before people split up: the beach and the bay can feel like two different places. Want something calmer? Landa is next door. See [best beaches in Ayia Napa](/guides/best-beaches-in-ayia-napa).

## Good to know

Sunbed prices and opening times aren't officially published. Ask before you settle in.
