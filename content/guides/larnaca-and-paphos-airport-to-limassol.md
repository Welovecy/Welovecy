Limassol sits between the island's two airports, so you can fly into either. The simplest way in is the airport shuttle.

## Limassol Airport Express

One operator, **Limassol Airport Express**, runs to Limassol from **both Larnaca and Paphos airports**.

- **In Limassol:** the main stop is **Havouzas**, which has **free parking** and **free Wi-Fi** for passengers. The operator also lists a **tourist-area stop opposite St Raphael**.
- **Times follow the flights.** The operator adjusts its timetable to arrivals, and runs a departure just after midnight.
- **Tickets:** at the **airport ticket desk**, or ahead online.
- **Fares:** the operator lists **€10 for adults** and **€5 for children aged 3 to 12**, on both airport routes.
- **Holidays:** timetables can change at Christmas and other holidays, so check the one for your date.

## Larnaca or Paphos?

Both work. Pick the airport by **when you land** and where your hotel is. Use [How far is it?](/distances) to compare the drive from each airport to Limassol.

## By taxi

Taxis wait outside arrivals at both airports. It's worth it if you land very late, have a lot of luggage, or your hotel is a long way from either shuttle stop. Agree the fare before you set off.

## Good to know

- Check the shuttle's timetable for your exact date and direction.
- Need step-free boarding? Ask the operator before you travel, as it isn't published.
