Limassol is the island's liveliest city, with a smart marina and a long seafront. But the best of it is older: a castle in the old town, and two ancient cities on the coast.

## Day one: the castle and the Old Port

### Limassol Castle and the Medieval Museum
The castle stands on **Richardou and Berengarias**, close to the **Old Port**, and houses the **Cyprus Medieval Museum**. It has had many lives: the Ottomans used it as a **garrison and a jail**, and under British rule it served as a **police station**. The museum opens on **weekdays from 09:00 to 17:00**. Check weekend and holiday hours before you go.

### The Old Port
A few minutes' walk away, the **Old Port** is the place for lunch by the water, and the old town's lanes start right behind it. [Limassol Old Port](/destinations/limassol)

## Day two: Kourion or Amathous

### Kourion
**19 km west** of Limassol, on the road towards Paphos, **Kourion** is one of the island's great ancient sites, with a **theatre** looking straight out to sea. Visit Cyprus lists entry at **€4.50**. Opening hours are **08:30 to 19:30 from 16 April to 15 September**, and **08:30 to 17:00 from 16 September to 15 April**, with holiday closures. The ground is uneven, and the site is **partly accessible** to wheelchairs.

### Amathous
Closer to town, **Amathous** is another ancient city on the coast. It's a good shorter option if you'd rather keep the afternoon for the beach.

## Beach time

**Dasoudi** beach is in **Germasogeia**, in the tourist area east of the centre.

## Our tip

Group the Old Port and the castle together, then give Kourion or Amathous its own half day. Go early in summer: there's little shade at either site.
