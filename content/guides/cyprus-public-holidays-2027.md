Cyprus follows the Orthodox calendar, so Easter and the holidays tied to it move every year. In 2027 Orthodox Easter falls on **2 May**, five weeks after Western Easter.

## All the dates

| Date | Day | Holiday |
|---|---|---|
| 1 January | Friday | New Year's Day |
| 6 January | Wednesday | Epiphany (Theophany) |
| 15 March | Monday | Green Monday (Clean Monday) |
| 25 March | Thursday | Greek Independence Day |
| 1 April | Thursday | Cyprus National Day |
| 30 April | Friday | Orthodox Good Friday |
| 1 May | Saturday | Labour Day |
| 3 May | Monday | Orthodox Easter Monday |
| 4 May | Tuesday | Easter Tuesday (banks only) |
| 21 June | Monday | Kataklysmos (Orthodox Whit Monday) |
| 15 August | Sunday | Dormition of the Virgin Mary |
| 1 October | Friday | Cyprus Independence Day |
| 28 October | Thursday | Ochi Day |
| 25 December | Saturday | Christmas Day |
| 26 December | Sunday | Boxing Day |

Many offices also close or finish early on **Christmas Eve, 24 December**. Check with the specific office or business.

## The long weekends

- **Green Monday, 13 to 15 March.** The start of Lent. Families head outdoors for picnics and kite flying, and tavernas serve meat-free meze.
- **Easter, 30 April to 3 May.** The biggest holiday of the year. Expect candlelit services on Saturday night and a long Sunday lunch.
- **Kataklysmos, 19 to 21 June.** The Festival of the Flood, celebrated by the sea, especially along the Larnaca seafront.
- **Independence Day, 1 to 3 October.** A Friday holiday, so a three-day weekend.

Four holidays fall on a weekend in 2027 (Labour Day, 15 August, Christmas Day and Boxing Day), so there are fewer long weekends than usual.

## What closes

- **Banks** close on every date above, including Easter Tuesday.
- **Pharmacies** close on public holidays, but there's always a pharmacy on duty in each town. See [pharmacies on duty](/guides/pharmacies-on-duty-in-cyprus).
- **Supermarkets and shops** in the resorts often stay open, especially in summer. Opening varies, so check locally.
- **Buses** usually run a Sunday or holiday timetable.
