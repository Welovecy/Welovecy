Nicosia is the capital, and the place to understand Cyprus's long story. Three of its best museums are a short walk apart.

## The Cyprus Museum

The island's main **archaeology museum**, at **1 Mouseiou**. If you only have time for one, make it this one.

- **Entry:** free.
- **Hours:** Tuesday to Friday **08:00 to 18:00**, Saturday **09:00 to 17:00**, Sunday **10:00 to 13:00**. **Closed on Mondays.**
- **Access:** an entrance ramp, chair lifts and accessible toilets.

## The Leventis Municipal Museum

At **15 to 17 Ippokratous**, in the old-town quarter of **Laiki Geitonia**, this museum tells the story of the city itself. It was named **European Museum of the Year in 1991**.

- **Hours:** Tuesday to Sunday, **10:00 to 16:30**.
- **Access:** facilities for disabled visitors are listed.

## The A. G. Leventis Gallery

Not to be confused with the museum above, the gallery is at **5 A. G. Leventis**. It holds three collections: the **Cyprus Collection**, the **Greek Collection** and the **Paris Collection** of European art.

## Putting it together

1. **Morning:** the Cyprus Museum, while you're fresh.
2. **Lunch:** in the old town, around Laiki Geitonia. [Laiki Geitonia](/destinations/nicosia)
3. **Afternoon:** the Leventis Municipal Museum, or the Gallery if art's more your thing.

Our tip: choose one main museum and fit a second around a coffee in the old town. Three in a day is a lot.

## Good to know

- Check holiday opening before you go. Mondays are tricky: the Cyprus Museum and the Municipal Museum are both closed.
- Nicosia is inland, so it's hotter in summer and cooler in winter than the coast.
