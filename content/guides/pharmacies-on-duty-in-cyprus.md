Pharmacies in Cyprus keep normal shop hours and close for a break in the afternoon. Outside those hours a rota of **pharmacies on duty** (in Greek, *διημερεύοντα φαρμακεία*) stays open in every town, so you're never far from one.

## When the duty pharmacies are open

The rota is set by law. On its duty day a pharmacy must open:

| Day | Duty hours |
|---|---|
| Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday | 13:30 to 15:00 and 18:30 to 22:00 |
| Wednesday and Saturday | 13:30 to 22:00 |
| Sunday and public holidays | 08:00 to 22:00 |

After 22:00 in winter, the duty pharmacist can still be called for an urgent prescription. The phone number is posted at the pharmacy.

## How to find one tonight

1. **Check We Love Cyprus.** [Pharmacies on duty tonight](/pharmacies-on-duty) shows today's rota for every area, with phone numbers and directions.
2. **Look in any pharmacy window.** Every pharmacy, open or closed, must display the names, addresses and phone numbers of the pharmacies on duty in a lit window by the door. Your nearest pharmacy is the quickest place to check.
3. **Check the official rota.** The Ministry of Health's Pharmaceutical Services publishes the full rota for each district (Nicosia, Limassol, Larnaca, Famagusta and Paphos), season by season. Ayia Napa, Protaras and Paralimni are on the Famagusta list.
4. **Ask at your hotel reception.** They usually have the day's list.

On We Love Cyprus, every hotel page also shows the nearest pharmacy and how far it is.

## Good to know

- **Bring your prescription,** or the generic name of your medicine. Brand names can differ from home.
- **Pharmacists in Cyprus can advise on minor problems** such as sunburn, insect bites, stomach upsets and allergies. It's a good first stop before a doctor.
- **In an emergency, don't wait for a pharmacy.** Call **112** or **199**, or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.
