Ayia Napa has the liveliest nights in Cyprus. But it isn't only clubs: there's a whole range, from a quiet taverna to a beach party.

## Where it happens

### Around the square
The heart of Ayia Napa's nightlife is the area **around the old monastery and the town square**, **packed with restaurants, bars and clubs**. Most nights start and finish here.

### Nissi Beach
In summer, **Nissi** has its own **music, bars and beach parties**. See the [Nissi Beach guide](/guides/nissi-beach-guide).

### Something calmer
The municipality's own list includes **pubs, tavernas and cafés** as well as clubs, with **live music and dancing** in places. And **many hotels** put on **live entertainment** every evening.

## Getting back to your hotel

Ayia Napa's hotels are spread out along the beaches, so plan the trip home before you go out.

- **Bus:** **OSEA** publishes the resort timetables. Check the last bus for your stop.
- **Taxi:** town taxis **use meters** and can be **booked by phone**.
- **Walking:** fine from the centre and Nissi Avenue. Further out, the walk can be longer than it looks.

## Our tips

- Agree a **meeting point** with your group before the night starts.
- Choosing where to stay? Check the **walk from the square back to your hotel** first. On We Love Cyprus, every hotel shows it.
- Venues change their opening dates, entry rules and prices through the season. Check each one's own page.
