Between Paphos and Limassol, a great sea stack rises from the waves. This is where, according to legend, the goddess of love first came ashore.

## The story

**Petra tou Romiou** is a natural **rock formation** on the **southwest coast** of the Paphos district. In **myth**, it's the birthplace of **Aphrodite**, **born from the sea foam** and **carried to the beach on a shell**.

The name itself comes from a different story. **Petra tou Romiou** means **Rock of the Greek**, and it's tied to the legendary Byzantine hero **Digenis Akritas**.

The site is part of the **Aphrodite Cultural Route**, which links places across Cyprus connected with the goddess. [Aphrodite's Rock](/places/aphrodite-rock)

## Visiting

It's on the coast road between **Paphos and Limassol**, so it makes an easy stop on a drive between the two. Allow extra time if you want photos from more than one viewpoint: the view from above is as good as the one from the beach.

## Swimming

The beach is pebbly, and the sea here can be rough. The **Marine Police** advise always **swimming in designated swimming areas**. Follow the signs, and check the conditions and whether there's a lifeguard before you go in.

## Good to know

Public transport and step-free access aren't confirmed for the site. Check before you go if you need either.
