Skiing in the morning and lunch by the sea in the afternoon. It's possible in Cyprus, in a good winter.

## Where

The slopes are on **Mount Olympus**, in the Troodos mountains, at **1,951 metres** above sea level, the highest point on the island. [Troodos](/destinations/troodos)

## When

The season usually starts at the **beginning of January** and ends at the **end of March**. It all depends on the snow, which varies a lot from year to year.

## The slopes

The **Cyprus Ski Club** lists **four lifts**, including:

- a **chairlift** on **Zeus**
- a **T-bar** on **Aphrodite**
- a **T-bar** on **Hermes**

When they're running, the lifts start at **09:00**.

## Before you go

- **Check the ski club** for whether the lifts are open, and for equipment hire and prices.
- **Check the snow forecast** on the ski club's weather page, and the Department of Meteorology.
- **Check the roads.** Snow and wind can close the mountain roads as well as the lifts.

## Our tip

Have a backup plan: if the lifts aren't running, a village lunch or a lower walk still makes a great day. See the [Troodos villages day trip](/guides/troodos-villages-day-trip).
