Ayia Napa's west coast is one long run of sandy bays, joined by a coastal path. You can pick one for the day, or walk between three before lunch.

## Nissi Beach: the famous one

About **600 metres** of soft sand in a natural bay, with the little island that gives it its name ("nissi" is Greek for island). It's the liveliest beach in Ayia Napa, with music and beach events in summer. Come early if you want a sunbed near the water. [Nissi Beach](/places/nissi)

## Landa: the calmer neighbour

Officially **halfway between Nissi Bay and Makronissos**, Landa is about **400 metres** long. Same golden sand, a slower pace. It's our pick when you want Nissi's water without Nissi's crowds.

## Vathia Gonia: small and easy

Just **east of Nissi Bay**, Vathia Gonia is a **300-metre** beach with parking nearby. Its Visit Cyprus listing gives lifeguards from **April to October**. The coastal path runs from here towards the harbour in one direction and Makronissos in the other.

## Makronissos: sand and history

Makronissos has wide, sandy beaches, and right beside them the **Makronissos Tombs**, an ancient rock-cut cemetery you can wander between swims. [Makronissos](/places/makronissos)

## Konnos Bay: the picture-postcard cove

Strictly speaking it's on the road between Cape Greco and Protaras, but it's a short hop from Ayia Napa. A sheltered cove with pine-covered slopes and very clear water. Parking fills up early in summer. [Konnos Bay](/places/konnos)

## Which one for you?

- **Lively, with music and a crowd:** Nissi.
- **Same sand, more space:** Landa.
- **Families with small children:** a beach with lifeguards on duty. Check the flag and the lifeguard tower when you arrive.
- **Snorkelling and photos:** Konnos, and the rocky stretches towards Cape Greco.

## Good to know

- Lifeguard seasons are published per beach, but cover on any given day can change. Check the flag and the tower on arrival.
- Sunbed and umbrella prices aren't officially published. Ask before you settle in.
