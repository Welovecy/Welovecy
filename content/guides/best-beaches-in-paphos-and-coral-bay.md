Much of the Paphos coast is rocky, which makes the sandy beaches all the more precious. Here are the ones worth planning a day around.

## Coral Bay: the classic beach day

In **Pegeia**, north of Paphos, Coral Bay has **soft white sand and shallow water**, with **rocky headlands** at either end. It's the beach most people picture when they think of Paphos, and it deserves a full day. [Coral Bay](/destinations/paphos/coral-bay)

**By bus:** route **615** starts at **Tombs of the Kings station** and runs along Coral Bay Avenue.

## Geroskipou: the family beach close to town

Just east of Paphos, Geroskipou's municipal beach is **400 metres** of sand with **calm, shallow water**. It has a **children's playground**, showers and changing rooms, so it's an easy choice with little ones.

## Faros

**Faros** is on the Paphos tourist board's list of beaches, and an option if you want somewhere nearer the town.

## Which one for you?

- **A whole day on the sand:** Coral Bay.
- **Young children, short on time:** Geroskipou.
- **Staying in Kato Paphos and want a quick swim:** a nearer town beach, and save Coral Bay for a full day.

## Good to know

- "Calm" describes the usual conditions, not every day. Follow the beach flags and check lifeguard cover when you arrive.
- Sunbed prices and opening hours aren't officially published. Ask before you settle.
- Going by bus? Check the time of the last 615 back.
