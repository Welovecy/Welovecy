A car opens up the mountains, the villages and the quieter beaches. If you're used to driving on the left, Cyprus will feel familiar. If not, give yourself a gentle first day.

## The rules that catch people out

- **Drive on the left.**
- **Kilometres, not miles.** Distances and speed limits are in km and km/h.
- **Seat belts for everyone,** front and back.
- **No handheld phones** while driving.
- **Babies in a rear-facing seat in the front:** only if the front passenger airbag is switched off.

## Hiring a car: what to check before you sign

Rental terms vary a lot between companies, so get these confirmed in writing:

1. **Your licence is accepted,** and any conditions on **age** or how long you've held it. The tourist board flags extra conditions for some younger drivers.
2. **The deposit and the excess,** and what the cover actually includes.
3. **Any roads you're not allowed to drive on.**
4. **Child seats,** if you need them.
5. **The return.** The European Consumer Centre Cyprus specifically warns about **returning a car outside business hours**. Find out what happens, and photograph the car and the fuel gauge when you drop it off.

## If something goes wrong

- **Emergency:** call **112**.
- **On the motorway:** there are **emergency phones** along the route.
- **Breakdown:** private breakdown services operate around the clock. Your hire company will tell you which one to call.
