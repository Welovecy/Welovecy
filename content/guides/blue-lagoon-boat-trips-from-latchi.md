The Akamas peninsula has no proper roads along its coast, so the best way to see it is from the sea. The boats from Latchi do exactly that, with a swim in the clearest water on the island.

## The trip

- **Departure:** **Latchi Harbour**.
- **The route:** along the **Akamas coastline**, past the **Baths of Aphrodite** and **Agios Georgios island**.
- **The highlight:** the boat **drops anchor in the Blue Lagoon** for a swim.

## Times and prices

Several boats run from Latchi. As one example, **Crystal Sea** publishes three sailings a day:

- **10:30 to 13:15**
- **14:00 to 17:00**
- **17:00 to 20:00**

It lists **€30 for adults** and **€15 for children under 12**, with seasonal fruit on board. **Private trips** are available on request, priced individually.

All sailings depend on the **weather and the sea**. Check on the day.

## Before you book

- Ask **where exactly the boat boards** in the harbour.
- Ask **how you get into the water** at the swim stop, and back out. Usually it's a ladder.
- **Step-free boarding** isn't published. Ask the operator directly.
- Follow the crew's instructions at the swim stop.

## Make a day of it

Combine the boat with lunch in Latchi and an afternoon in Polis. See [Polis and Latchi in one day](/guides/polis-and-latchi-in-one-day).
