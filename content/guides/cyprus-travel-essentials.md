Everything you need to know before you pack.

## Plugs and power

Cyprus uses plugs with **three square pins**, the same as the UK, and the supply is **230 volts**. Coming from the UK, you won't need an adapter. Coming from mainland Europe or the US, bring one.

## Money

- **Currency:** the **euro (€)**.
- **Cards:** all major credit cards are accepted in most places. Carry some cash for small cafés, kiosks and buses: some local buses take cash only.
- **Ask for the price in euros** when you pay by card, so your own bank does the conversion.

## Tipping

Tipping in Cyprus is **at the customer's discretion**. There's no fixed rule. If you've had good service, leaving something is appreciated.

## Emergency numbers

One number covers it all: **112** for the **police**, an **ambulance** or the **fire service**.

**112 is free to call**, from any phone.

## Pharmacies

Pharmacies are marked with a **green cross**. Outside normal hours, a rota of pharmacies stays open late. See [pharmacies on duty in Cyprus](/guides/pharmacies-on-duty-in-cyprus).

## Our tip

Save your accommodation's address, in Greek and English, on your phone before you land. It helps with taxis, deliveries and anyone you need to call.
