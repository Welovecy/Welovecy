From the outside, they look like barns with steep wooden roofs. Step inside and every wall is covered in paintings, some of them nearly a thousand years old.

## The ten churches

The **Painted Churches in the Troodos Region** are a single UNESCO World Heritage Site, a **complex of ten monuments**, all **richly decorated with murals**. They include:

- **Panagia Phorviotissa (Asinou)**
- **Agios Nikolaos tis Stegis**, near Kakopetria
- **Agios Ioannis Lampadistis** monastery
- **Panagia tou Arakou**
- **Archangel Michael**
- **Panagia Podithou**
- **Stavros tou Agiasmati** (the Holy Cross)
- **Ayia Sotira** at **Palaichori**, added to the listing later

UNESCO's own maps show exactly where each one is.

## Planning your route

The churches are spread across the mountains, on slow roads. A good half day pairs **Asinou** with **Agios Nikolaos tis Stegis** and the **Kakopetria** area, if both are open.

Our tip: **two churches**, with real time inside each, beat a long checklist.

## Before you go

- **Opening times vary,** and some churches are opened by a local keyholder. Check each one with the Department of Antiquities, or locally, before you set out.
- **These are places of worship.** Dress modestly, and check the rules on photography and admission at each one.
