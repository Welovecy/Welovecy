You've landed. Here's the simplest way to get from the arrivals hall to your sunbed, depending on when you land and where you're staying.

## The three ways to do it

| | Shuttle | Bus | Taxi |
|---|---|---|---|
| **From** | Stop outside arrivals | Airport bus stop | Rank outside arrivals |
| **Changes** | None | One or two | None |
| **Time to Ayia Napa** | About 45 minutes | 2 hours or more | About 45 minutes |
| **Best for** | Most people | Light luggage, daytime, tight budget | Late flights, families, groups |

## By shuttle: the easy option

The **Kapnos Airport Shuttle** leaves from its own stop directly outside the arrivals building, with a counter in the arrivals hall. It doesn't go door to door. It stops at fixed points:

- **Ayia Napa:** Ayia Napa Square (Arch. Makariou III 19) and Nissi Avenue 80, outside the Sunwing Sandy Bay hotel. About 45 minutes in normal traffic.
- **Protaras:** Pernera Avenue, about 150 metres from the Windmills hotel, and the Protaras Avenue stop by McDonald's. About an hour.

Kapnos runs every day, roughly from the early hours until late evening, but the timetable changes with the season, so check the departure that fits your flight. You can book online up to an hour before departure, at the airport counter or from the driver. In June, July and August, book a few days ahead, because same-day seats can sell out.

When you're booking, Kapnos lists fares from €13 for an adult to Ayia Napa, and €15 for an adult and €5 for a child (2 to 12) to Protaras. Prices can move with demand.

> **From the stop to your hotel:** open your hotel's page on We Love Cyprus to see how far it is from the nearest stop, with a walking time or a taxi estimate.

## By bus: the budget option

There's no direct public bus from the airport to Ayia Napa or Protaras, so this takes a change in Larnaca.

1. **Airport to Larnaca:** take a Larnaca town bus from the airport stop. Route **425** links the airport with Mackenzie, Finikoudes and Larnaca bus station.
2. **Larnaca to Ayia Napa and Paralimni:** the **Intercity** bus leaves from **Finikoudes (Larnaca Marina)**. It takes about an hour and 15 minutes, calling at stops along the Ayia Napa coast before finishing in Paralimni.
3. **For Protaras:** change in Paralimni to a local OSEA bus, or take a short taxi.

Intercity buses run every day, with the first bus from Larnaca at 06:00 and the last at 21:00. A one-way ticket on this route is €5.

This works best by day, with a small bag and time to spare. If your flight lands in the evening, the last connections can leave you stuck in Larnaca.

## By taxi: the late-flight option

Taxis wait at the rank outside arrivals. In 2021 the Ministry of Transport set fixed airport fares. For up to four people, it listed Ayia Napa at €50 by day and €60 at night (20:30 to 06:00), and Protaras at €55 by day and €65 at night. Fares may have changed since, so **agree the price with the driver before you set off**. You can also pre-book a private transfer, which is handy with children or lots of luggage.

## Which one should you choose?

- **Landing between breakfast and evening, travelling light:** shuttle. It's the best balance of price and ease.
- **Landing late at night:** taxi or a pre-booked transfer. Check the last shuttle for your date before you rely on it.
- **Staying in Protaras or Kapparis with children:** shuttle to the nearest stop, then a short taxi if your hotel isn't close. Or a taxi all the way.
- **Backpacking or staying a while:** the bus. It's slow, but it's the cheapest.

## Before you go

- Check your shuttle or bus times for your actual travel date. Timetables change between summer and winter.
- Save your hotel's address in Greek and English, in case your driver asks.
