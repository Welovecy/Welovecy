Larnaca is often the first and last thing visitors see of Cyprus, on the way to or from the airport. Give it a day and you'll find one of the island's most likeable towns.

## Morning: the old town on foot

### Saint Lazarus
Start at the **Church of Saint Lazarus**, the town's best-known landmark. Check access times before you go, as it's a working church. [Saint Lazarus](/places/st-lazarus)

### The market and Zouchouri Square
A short walk away, the **municipal market** sits by **Zouchouri Square**. A good place for coffee and a look at what's in season.

### Finikoudes
Then down to **Finikoudes**, the palm-lined seafront promenade, for a swim or a long lunch. The open-air **Pattichion Municipal Theatre** is nearby, too. [Finikoudes](/places/finikoudes)

## Afternoon: the Salt Lake

Just outside town, the **Larnaca Salt Lake** is Cyprus's **second-largest salt lake**, covering about **2.2 square kilometres**. In winter it's known for its **flamingos**, though how many you'll see depends on the year and the water. A **nature trail** runs around the area. [Salt Lake](/places/salt-lake)

### Hala Sultan Tekke
On the lake's shore stands **Hala Sultan Tekke**. Entry is **free**, and it's **partly accessible** to wheelchairs. Opening hours are **08:30 to 19:30 from 16 April to 15 September**, and **08:30 to 17:00 from 16 September to 15 April**. Holidays can change this. [Hala Sultan Tekke](/places/hala-sultan)

## Getting around

The town centre is easy on foot. Bus **425** runs between the **airport**, the hotels, **Mackenzie** and **Finikoudes**, so Larnaca works well without a car. The Salt Lake is a short taxi ride from town.

## Our tip

Do the market and the church before lunch, while it's cool, and save the lake for the late-afternoon light.
