Cyprus is a brilliant family destination: shallow sandy beaches, warm water and short drives. When the kids need a break from the beach, here's where to go.

## Ayia Napa

- **WaterWorld:** the big waterpark day out. [WaterWorld](/places/waterworld)
- **Parko Paliatso:** a **luna park** on **Nissi Avenue**, with rides that come alive in the evening.
- **Beaches:** for families, choose one with lifeguards on duty. See [best beaches in Ayia Napa](/guides/best-beaches-in-ayia-napa).

## Protaras

- **Ocean Aquarium:** at **19 Cavo Greco, Protara Avenue**, a good choice for the hottest hours of the day.
- **Fig Tree Bay:** lifeguards from April to October, toilets and changing rooms.

## Paphos

- **Geroskipou beach:** calm, shallow water and a **children's playground**. See [best beaches in Paphos and Coral Bay](/guides/best-beaches-in-paphos-and-coral-bay).

## When it's too hot, or raining

The Visit Cyprus family directory lists outings by resort, including:

- **Bowling**
- **Indoor playgrounds**
- **Mini golf**
- **CyHerbia,** a botanical park and maze

## Our tip

From the same hotel, plan one short outing (a morning or an evening) and one full-day option. That way you're never stuck deciding on the day.

## Good to know

Opening times, prices, ride height limits and accessibility vary by venue and season, so check with each place before you go.
