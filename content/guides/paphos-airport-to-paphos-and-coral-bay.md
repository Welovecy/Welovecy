Paphos Airport is small and close to town, so getting to your hotel is easy. The only question is which bus, and whether you need to change.

## Which bus goes where

| You're staying in | Take | Change? |
|---|---|---|
| Kato Paphos, the harbour, Poseidonos Avenue | **612** to Tombs of the Kings station | No |
| Paphos town centre, the old town | **613** to Karavella station | No |
| Chloraka, Kissonerga, Coral Bay | **612**, then **615** | Yes, at Tombs of the Kings station |

Both airport buses leave from the stop outside the terminal and run every day of the week.

## Bus 612: the tourist area

Bus 612 runs from the airport through Timi, Achelia and Geroskipou, then along **Poseidonos Avenue** past the Almyra hotel and into town, finishing at **Tombs of the Kings bus station** on Tombs of the Kings Avenue, opposite McDonald's.

It runs roughly every hour or so, from early morning until after midnight. The current timetable lists the last bus from the airport at 01:00. Check the times for your date on the Pafos Buses website.

## Bus 613: the town centre

Bus 613 goes through Timi, Achelia and Geroskipou to **Karavella station**, in the centre of Paphos, where most regional buses start. It's the one to take for the old town and the shops. There are around a dozen departures a day, with the current last bus from the airport at midnight.

## Coral Bay: one change

There's no direct bus from the airport to Coral Bay. Take **612** to Tombs of the Kings station, then **615**, which runs along Tombs of the Kings Avenue and the Chloraka to Kissonerga coast road to **Coral Bay**.

The 615 is frequent: every 10 to 15 minutes during the day, and every 20 minutes on summer evenings (every 30 minutes from December to March), with the last bus leaving Tombs of the Kings at around midnight or later in summer. Allow an hour or more for the whole trip.

## Tickets

Pay the driver. A one-way ticket on Paphos district buses is **€2**, or **€3 after 21:00**, and luggage is included. Children up to 5 travel free and children up to 12 pay half. If you'll ride a lot, a day ticket is **€6.50**. On the Coral Bay route, expect to buy a second ticket when you change.

## By taxi

The taxi rank is outside arrivals. In 2021 the Ministry of Transport set fixed airport fares and listed "Paphos/Peyia" (Peyia covers Coral Bay) at €20 by day and €25 at night for up to four people. More recent guides quote higher prices, so **ask the driver for the fixed fare before you set off**. It's the sensible choice after midnight, with small children or with lots of bags.

## Which one should you choose?

- **Staying in Kato Paphos or by the harbour:** bus 612. It's cheap, direct and drops you in the middle of the tourist area.
- **Staying in the old town:** bus 613.
- **Staying in Coral Bay with luggage:** a taxi is far simpler than changing buses.
- **Landing after midnight:** taxi.
