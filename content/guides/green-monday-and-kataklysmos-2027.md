Two holidays you won't find in many other countries' calendars, and both are a lovely time to be in Cyprus.

## Green Monday: 15 March 2027

**Green Monday**, also called **Clean Monday**, falls **50 days before Orthodox Easter**. In 2027 that's **Monday 15 March**. It marks the start of Lent, and it's a **public holiday** and a **bank holiday**.

Traditionally, families head out to the countryside for picnics, and flying kites is part of the day.

## Kataklysmos: 21 June 2027

**Kataklysmos**, the **Festival of the Flood**, falls on **Pentecost Monday** (Orthodox Whit Monday). In 2027 that's **Monday 21 June**, again a public and bank holiday.

It's celebrated by the sea, above all in **Larnaca**. In 2026 the town's Kataklysmos programme was held on the **Finikoudes** seafront. [Finikoudes](/places/finikoudes)

The 2027 programme will be published by Larnaca Municipality nearer the time.

## Planning around them

- **Banks are closed** on both days.
- **Shops:** opening hours vary by type and location.
- **Buses and roads:** look out for diversions around events.

Check the local listings in the week before each holiday. See [Cyprus public holidays 2027](/guides/cyprus-public-holidays-2027) for the full year.
