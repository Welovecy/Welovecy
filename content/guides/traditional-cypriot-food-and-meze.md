If you only eat one meal like a local in Cyprus, make it a meze. Come hungry, and don't rush.

## What is meze?

**Meze** (or **mezedes**) is a **meal of many small dishes**, **shared around the table**. They arrive in waves, and it usually **begins with olives** and dips with bread. Meat or fish follow, in more rounds than you'd expect.

## Dishes to know

### Dips
- **Tahini:** a **sesame** dip.
- **Skordalia:** **potato and garlic**.
- **Taramosalata:** made with **fish roe**.

### Cheese and cured meats
- **Halloumi:** the island's own cheese, traditionally stored **in salt with mint**. Try it grilled.
- **Lountza:** **cured pork loin**.

### Something heartier
- **Kleftiko:** **slow-cooked lamb**, falling off the bone.
- **Elliopittes:** little **olive pies**.

## How to order

1. **Ask what's on today.** Many places serve whatever's fresh.
2. **Ask about pace.** The kitchen can bring the dishes slowly, so you're not swamped.
3. **Fish or meat?** Many places offer both kinds of meze. Choose one for the table.
4. **Pace yourself.** The best dishes often come later.

Portion sizes, prices and menus vary from place to place, so ask before you order.

## Allergies

Tell your server about any allergy, and ask what each dish contains, including cross-contact in the kitchen. Remember that **tahini contains sesame** and **taramosalata contains fish roe**.

## At Easter

Look out for **flaounes**, the Easter cheese pastries. See [Easter in Cyprus 2027](/guides/easter-in-cyprus-2027).
