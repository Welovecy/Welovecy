Winter in Cyprus isn't summer, but it isn't winter as northern Europe knows it either. Think walking weather, quiet ruins and long lunches in the sun.

## What to expect

- **When:** winter runs from **December to February**.
- **Temperature:** mild, with **January** usually the coolest month.
- **Rain:** winter is the rainy season, so expect some wet days.
- **Sunshine:** still around **six hours a day** in December and January.
- **February:** changeable, but it's when the **almond trees blossom**.

See [Cyprus in January](/weather/january), [February](/weather/february) and [December](/weather/december) for the averages, area by area.

## Can you swim?

The open sea averages about **16 or 17°C** from January to March. Bracing for most, but plenty of locals swim all year.

## Snow in the mountains

The coast stays mild, but **Troodos** gets snow. Some winters there's enough for skiing on Mount Olympus. See [skiing on Mount Olympus](/guides/skiing-on-mount-olympus).

The coast and the mountains can be very different on the same day. Check mountain road conditions before you drive up.

## What's good in winter

- **The ancient sites:** cool and quiet. Try [Paphos](/guides/things-to-do-in-paphos) or [Kourion](/guides/things-to-do-in-limassol).
- **Walking:** the coastal paths at [Cape Greco](/guides/cape-greco-sea-caves-and-walks) are at their best.
- **The villages:** a [Troodos day trip](/guides/troodos-villages-day-trip), with a fire and a meze to finish.
- **Nicosia:** museums for a rainy day. See [one day in Nicosia](/guides/one-day-in-nicosia).

## Our tip

Choose a base where a rainy morning still gives you something good to do on foot: a town with museums and cafés beats a quiet beach resort.

## Good to know

Some resort hotels, bars and boat trips close for the winter, especially in Ayia Napa and Protaras. Check before you book.
