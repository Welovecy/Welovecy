# welovecy.com: working notes

The We Love Cyprus guide. Republic of Cyprus only, never the north. Owner: Dami Adufe, run by The One Experience Adufe Ltd.

## Rules for any change
- Copy: no em dashes, human tone, short headlines, price never the lead. Taxi fares are always a range ("€X–Y by taxi (estimate)"). Walking, driving and taxi figures are labelled as estimates.
- Every fact on a page has a source; official registers and tourism sources first. Never invent venues, figures or claims.
- Only show photos we're allowed to use (CC licences with credit, or the venue's permission). Credits live in `data/images.json` and show only on /credits and in the footer line (never on each page). Keys: `place-<slug>`, `beach-<listing id>`, `poi-<listing id>` (sights, trails, museums), `biz-<id>`, `dest-<id>`. Commons URLs without tracking parameters; `scripts/check/image_urls.js` checks they load. Commons photos are temporary until our own photography replaces them.
- Mobile first: most visitors are on phones (92%). Check 390px width and that nothing moves after load (layout shift).
- Before pushing: `npm run typecheck`, `npx vitest run`, `npm run build`. Push to `main` deploys to production on Vercel; branches get preview URLs.
- Commit as `We Love Cyprus <welovecy@users.noreply.github.com>`.

## How we ship (agreed 28 Nov 2026)
- Fast lane, goes live without asking, listed in a daily changelog to Dami: bug fixes; official-source facts for records we already have (opening hours, access, event dates); photos that pass the Commons licence check; changes to check scripts. Undo is one Vercel rollback.
- Approval lane, waits for "go live": anything visual or design, partners and anything commercial, new kinds of pages, anything about money. Batch these into one preview, one screenshot sheet and one go live a day where possible.
- Research from ChatGPT arrives as a staging sheet (Staging id, Category, Matched site record, Evidence URL, details). Import matched official rows with a script and list the rest for review; never hand-edit row by row.
- Other boat operators stay off the site while CY Cruises is our boat partner, unless Dami says otherwise.

## Stack
Next.js 15 App Router, React 19, TypeScript, Vitest. Static pages (about 3,000) plus ISR for the homepage and duty pharmacy pages (revalidate 600). No database. Vercel Pro, Web Analytics (custom events), Speed Insights. Installable web app (`src/app/manifest.ts`, `public/sw.js`).

## Data (all in `data/`)
- `businesses.json`: every licensed business from the official registers, plus `extra_businesses.json` (our own additions, ids `wlc-0001`…; merged by `scripts/data/apply_pins.py`). Fields: `ll` pin, `ls` pin source (ov/osm/us), `pr` precision (approx/area; area pins are kept out of "what's around"), `vt` venue type, `cz` cuisines, `in`/`fl`/`hrs` for venues inside a hotel.
- `pois.json`: beaches, sights, towns. `attractions.json`: museums, family days out, venues, malls and marinas (built by `scripts/data/build_attractions.py` from the OSM extract made by `attractions.yml`, saved to `data/work/osm_attractions.json`); each has sources, an entrance pin where mapped, and `inside` = licensed venues within it (they get `at` and the attraction's pin). Kinds: sight, family, activity (dive centres, water sports), nature (walks and nature trails, with `len`, `grade` 1 to 3 from the Department of Forests scale, `place` = the place page it starts from), info (tourist information offices). `notice` on an attraction or a place (guide.json) shows a closure banner and Aphrodite says it first. New places by name: run `osm-lookup.yml` with a pattern, then add to SPEC. `knowledge.json`: Know before you go facts (sec, q keywords, t text, src), shown on `/know-before-you-go` and searched by Aphrodite's `know` tool (`src/lib/knowledge.ts`); recheck rules that change (entry, drones, e-scooters, roaming) monthly. Sections also hold areas (which area suits which trip), villages, history (page /cyprus-history) and living (expat facts by topic, page /living-in-cyprus; Aphrodite always adds "check with the department"). Indoor categories for rainy days: `src/lib/indoor.ts` (`find_places` with `indoor:true`). `motoring.json`: petrol stations (k fuel: brand, fuels, h24) and car hire offices (k carhire), built by `scripts/data/build_motoring.py` from the OSM extract made by `motoring.yml` (saved to `data/work/osm_motoring.json`, which also holds EV chargers for later); merged into POIS; page `/petrol` (`PetrolFinder`, nearest by drive time) and essentials pages. Fuel prices: the government Retail Fuel Price Observatory is behind a gov.cy login, so no prices until the Consumer Protection Service agrees. `aliases.json`: other names by id, used by search and Aphrodite. `content.json`: destinations, places, experiences. `images.json`: photos and credits. `duty.json`: pharmacy rota (built by `scripts/content/duty.py`; 2026-27 winter rota, summer rota needed by April 2027). `bus.json`, `climate.json`, `distances.json`, `quiz.json`.
- Pins by phone: `scripts/data/match_phone.py <overture places json>` matches unpinned records to Overture places by registered phone (and pharmacists by name), only near their own town and never north of the line; results go into `data/work/overture_pins.json`, which `apply_pins.py` reads. Second pass: `scripts/data/match_second.py data/work/overture_places.json` (newest Overture release, fetched by `overture.yml` to the `overture` branch) matches missing and rough pins by phone (names must also look alike) then name, near the rough pin; writes `data/work/overture_pins2.json`, applied by `apply_pins.py`.
- Rebuild data: `npm run data`. Pins come from OpenStreetMap via GitHub Actions (`osm-refresh.yml`), because this workspace can't reach Overpass or Nominatim. `coast.yml` extracts coastline and roads for map graphics into the `coastline` branch.

## Key code
- `src/lib/geo.ts`: distances, walk/drive minutes, `taxiRange` (+25% margin), `howFar`.
- `src/lib/views.ts`: `aroundData` ("what's around"), `insideOf` (venues in a hotel).
- `src/lib/duty.ts`: duty pharmacies; `dirFor` searches Google Maps for "Φαρμακείο + street + town" (rota pins are approximate).
- `src/lib/going.ts`: eat and drink pages by area and cuisine. `src/lib/hotelsNear.ts`: hotels near beaches.
- `src/components/HomeHero.tsx`: homepage video, live strip, shortcuts. `HeroDemo.tsx`: the "Staying at…?" demo (keep its title at a fixed height).
- `src/components/InstallPrompt.tsx`: Add to Home Screen nudge (Android install button via beforeinstallprompt, iPhone Safari 3 steps), from the 2nd visit, after the cookie choice, never when installed or dismissed (localStorage `wlc-install`). Events: Install shown / accepted / dismissed, Installed. Aphrodite's face: `public/aphrodite-96.webp` and `-192.webp` in the `Orb`.
- `src/components/Track.tsx`: tap tracking (Call, Map, Facebook, Outbound, Staying here, Email). `Consent.tsx`: cookie banner; Microsoft Clarity (project `zitl4a1c8f`) loads only after Accept.
- Aphrodite, the AI guide: `src/components/Ask.tsx` (orb button, homepage `AskBar`, glass sheet, cards; open from anywhere with `openAphrodite(q)`), `src/app/api/ask/route.ts` (Claude Haiku 5.5, persona and rules, cards, follow-ups, rate limit, `Ask` analytics event), `src/lib/ask/tools.ts` (tools over our data plus a sitemap search, so new data and pages are known on the next deploy). Needs `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` in Vercel (Production and Preview); spend limit set in the Claude Console.
- Explore: `src/app/explore` (Places, `/events`, `/festivals`), `src/components/Explore.tsx`, feature card in `src/lib/explore.ts`. Events in `data/events.json` via `src/lib/events.ts`: every event needs a checked source_url (official or organiser first); unconfirmed dates go in as `dates_tbc` with `usual`. Add to calendar via `/api/ics/[id]`. Aphrodite reads events through the `events` tool.
- Partners (advertising): `data/partners.json` (public facts only, never fees or contracts), `src/lib/partners.ts` (`partnerFor` decides eligibility in code by where the visitor is staying), unlisted noindex pages at `/partners/[id]` (not in sitemap or search), `PartnerSlot` on matching airport guides, Aphrodite tool `transfer_partner` with a once-per-conversation check. What a partner sells (CY Cruises' cruises: name, length, route, includes, public price, photo, link) lives in `data/partner_offers.json` (never fees); the partner page shows it as cards (the template for future partners), photos in `public/img/partners/<partner>/`, fetched by `partner-images.yml` (push `scripts/content/partner_images.txt` to the `partner-images` branch). Aphrodite's `boat_trips` tool returns CY Cruises' range for boat questions; `partnerLine` adds a line when she forgets. Always labelled "Advertising partner". Events: Partner shown / book / call / page / website / email.
- Share images: `src/app/api/og/route.tsx` draws a 1200x630 card per page from `ogFor(path)` in `src/lib/og.ts` (our data only, never text from the URL; photo or the island with a pin; fonts in `src/app/api/og/fonts`, JPEG/PNG only). Pages set `openGraph: ogMeta(path)`; bump `v` in `ogImage` when the design changes.
- `src/lib/site.ts`: emails (hello@ / business@), Facebook links.
